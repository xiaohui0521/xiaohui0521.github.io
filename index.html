About

I am a PhD researcher in Psychology at the University of Edinburgh, where I work under the supervision of Professor René Mõttus. I completed both my bachelor’s and master’s degrees at Hunan Normal University, where I was supervised by Professor Yanhui Xiang.

My research examines the links between romantic relationship status and life satisfaction, as well as the associations among income, personality, and life satisfaction. I am also conducting a meta-analysis of romantic relationship status and subjective well-being.
